## 1️⃣ Overview
The **Edtech_Analysis** project focuses on analyzing online recorded courses across multiple EdTech platforms to understand learner preferences, content demand, engagement patterns, and growth opportunities.  
The analysis helps an EdTech startup make **data-driven decisions** on course creation, instructor partnerships, language strategy, and skill-focused content expansion.

The final insights are presented using an **interactive Power BI dashboard** to support strategic planning.

---

## 2️⃣ Business Problem
With rapid growth in online education, EdTech companies face challenges such as:
- Identifying **high-demand categories and sub-categories**
- Understanding **viewer engagement drivers**
- Choosing the **right course types and languages**
- Selecting **top-performing instructors**
- Designing **optimal course duration and structure**

This project aims to uncover insights that enable the company to:
- Expand recorded lecture offerings effectively
- Improve learner engagement
- Align course content with job market needs

---

## 3️⃣ Dataset Overview
The dataset represents **online courses from various EdTech platforms** and provides a comprehensive view of the online learning ecosystem.

**Dataset Characteristics:**
- 📌 Total Records: **10,000+ rows**
- 📌 Format: CSV

## 5️⃣ Dashboard Insights (Key Findings)

### 📌 Course Type Popularity
- **Standalone courses dominate** the platform offerings
- Specializations and professional certificates have significantly lower counts
- Indicates learner preference for **short, focused learning**

---

### 📌 Category & Sub-Category Distribution
- **Data Science, Computer Science, and IT** have the highest number of courses
- Sub-categories like **Machine Learning, Business, Cloud Computing** show strong demand
- Suggests scope for deeper specialization in technical domains

---

### 📌 Average Views by Category
- **Data Science** ranks highest in average views
- Followed by **Information Technology** and **Computer Science**
- Personal Development and Language Learning show moderate engagement

---

### 📌 Demanding Skills Analysis
- Top skills include:
  - Python Programming
  - Data Analysis
  - Machine Learning
  - Artificial Intelligence
  - Business & Communication Skills
- Confirms strong alignment with **industry and job-market trends**

---

### 📌 Language Distribution
- **English dominates (~97%)** of course content
- Other languages like Spanish and French have very limited presence
- Indicates untapped opportunities for **regional language expansion**

---

### 📌 Subtitle Impact on Viewership
- Courses with **multiple subtitle languages attract higher average views**
- Subtitles significantly enhance **accessibility and global reach**

---

### 📌 Instructor Performance
- Several instructors consistently maintain **high ratings (~4.9)**
- Identifying top instructors per category helps in:
  - Strategic partnerships
  - Content collaboration
  - Quality benchmarking

---

### 📌 Course Duration vs Engagement
- Medium-length courses show **higher viewer engagement**
- Extremely long or very short courses tend to attract fewer viewers
- Optimal duration planning is critical for learner retention

---

### 📌 Skill Variety vs Viewership
- Categories offering a **broader range of skills** experience higher engagement
- Data Science leads in average skill count per course
- Skill diversity enhances perceived course value

---

## 6️⃣ Final Conclusion
The **Edtech_Analysis** highlights that learner engagement is driven by:
- High-demand technical categories
- Industry-relevant skills
- English-first content with subtitle support
- Optimal course duration
- High-quality instructors

### 🎯 Strategic Recommendations:
- Invest more in **Data Science, IT, and Computer Science**
- Expand **subtitle and multilingual support**
- Collaborate with **top-rated instructors**
- Focus on **skill-diverse, medium-length courses**
- Explore regional language opportunities for market expansion
